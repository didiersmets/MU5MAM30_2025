========================================
INITIAL CONDITION (Atmospheric-like Rossby waves)
========================================

3 * cos(16*y + 4*sin(3*atan2(z,x) + 2*y) + 5 *sin(7*atan2(z,x)))


========================================
THEORETICAL DESCRIPTION
========================================

This initial condition is designed to reproduce large-scale atmospheric 
Rossby wave patterns on a rotating sphere.

The structure is composed of three main elements:

1) Zonal structure (cos(16*y))
   This term generates smooth, large-scale bands similar to atmospheric 
   jet streams. These bands represent latitudinal variations in vorticity 
   that are typical of mid-latitude atmospheric circulation.

2) Longitudinal modulation (sin and atan2 terms)
   The functions involving atan2(z,x) introduce a dependence on longitude.
   This produces meanders and wave-like distortions of the zonal jets.
   These are analogous to planetary Rossby waves in the real atmosphere.

3) Nonlinear phase coupling
   The combination of multiple sinusoidal terms creates realistic 
   large-scale wave patterns without introducing high-frequency noise.
   This allows the simulation to remain numerically stable while still 
   generating dynamically rich structures.

========================================
WHY THIS INITIAL CONDITION IS INTERESTING
========================================

• It mimics the large-scale circulation of the Earth's atmosphere, 
  including jet streams and planetary waves.

• It allows the visualization of Rossby wave propagation, deformation,
  and interaction.

• It is smooth and well-behaved, which reduces the risk of numerical 
  instability while still producing meaningful nonlinear dynamics.

• It provides a good test case to study:
    - Rossby wave propagation
    - Wave breaking and mixing
    - Jet instability
    - Large-scale atmospheric variability

• The pattern evolves into vortices and filament structures when the 
  viscosity is sufficiently small, making it useful for studying 
  geophysical turbulence.

========================================
SUGGESTED PARAMETERS
========================================

For best results:
   viscosity (nu) ~ 1e-3 to 1e-2
   angular velocity ~ 0.1 to 0.3

These values typically produce a good balance between stability and 
rich atmospheric-like dynamics.
